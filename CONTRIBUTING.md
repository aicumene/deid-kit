# Contributing to deid-kit

deid-kit is offered under the AGPL and, separately, under a commercial license. That works only
while one party holds the rights to all of the code, so contributions are accepted under a
contributor license agreement (CLA): the contributor keeps the copyright in the contribution and
grants the copyright holder, Alexandra Bernadotte, a perpetual, worldwide, irrevocable,
royalty-free license to use, modify and sublicense it, including under commercial terms.

Before a pull request, open an issue to discuss the change; the CLA is sent there. Contributions
without a signed CLA cannot be merged.

Test data must be invented: no real names, addresses, identifiers or client material.
